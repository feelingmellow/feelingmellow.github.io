Your Guide to Traditional & Modern Preparation

What is Kava?

Kava is a traditional beverage made from the root of the Piper methysticum plant, native to the South Pacific Islands. It has been used for centuries in social and ceremonial gatherings for its relaxing and mildly euphoric effects.

Traditional Kava Preparation

You Will Need:

  • Medium grind kava root powder
  • Warm water
  • Strainer bag (muslin or fine mesh)
  • Large bowl

The Process:

  1. Measure 2-4 tablespoons of kava powder into the strainer bag.
  2. Place the bag in the bowl and add 8-12 ounces of warm water.
  3. Knead and squeeze the bag for 5-10 minutes to extract the kava.
  4. Remove the bag and enjoy the strained liquid.

This traditional method, while requiring a bit more effort, is considered by many to be the best way to experience kava's full effects.

Modern Kava Preparation (Blender Method)

For a quicker and easier preparation, you can use a blender.

You Will Need:

  • Medium grind kava root powder
  • Water
  • Blender
  • Strainer bag
  • Bowl

The Process:

  1. Add 2-4 tablespoons of kava powder and 8-12 ounces of water to the blender.
  2. Blend on high for 3-4 minutes.
  3. Strain the mixture through the strainer bag into a bowl.
  4. Serve and enjoy.

Tips for the Perfect Brew

  • Use quality kava: The variety and quality of kava greatly affect the final product.
  • Water temperature: Use warm water for traditional prep, room temperature for the blender method.
  • Strain well: A fine strainer bag is crucial for a smooth drink.
  • Enjoy responsibly: Kava has potent effects; consume in moderation.
