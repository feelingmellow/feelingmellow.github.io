How to Make Kava

Kava is a traditional drink made from the root of the kava plant. Follow these simple steps to prepare it:

  1. Measure the desired amount of kava powder (typically 2-4 tablespoons per serving).
  2. Place the powder into a strainer bag or muslin cloth.
  3. Add water (room temperature or slightly warm) to a bowl.
  4. Knead the kava in the water for 5-10 minutes, ensuring the liquid becomes cloudy.
  5. Strain out the kava pulp and enjoy your kava drink!

Enjoy responsibly and relax with the mellow effects of kava.
